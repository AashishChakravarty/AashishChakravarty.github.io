Hi — I'm Aashish, a software engineer based in Indore, India. Six-plus years in, I've worked across SaaS, IIoT, supply chain, real-time data, and AI tooling. I lean toward the backend: the systems, the pipelines, the things that have to keep running.

Most of my recent work is in Elixir & Phoenix on the BEAM, focused on MCP server infrastructure and AI-agent tooling — the plumbing that connects LLM clients to the rest of a stack.

I like owning features end-to-end: scoping with product, sketching the architecture, writing the code, reviewing PRs, mentoring teammates, running deploys. The interesting work is in the seams.

Outside of that, I've shipped real-time Kafka pipelines for sports-betting odds, IIoT dashboards for plant data, supply-chain traceability for the textile industry, and a farmer-focused mobile app that crossed 8K downloads.
